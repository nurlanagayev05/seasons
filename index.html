My primary-school years, around ages six to twelve, in Cologne on the Rhine.

Spring Summer Autumn Winter
01

Spring

Rain showers & new beginnings

Spring in Cologne: A Changing Landscape

It has been many years since the primary-school days of a little boy who grew up in Cologne on the Rhine. He remembers spring as having slowly but surely gotten better as the bare trees turned green. At the beginning of the season it could be cold in the mornings (5°C and below) and then warm up to a pleasant 15-20°C in the afternoons. The rain fell in light showers or as steady drizzle, but it never threatened to cause flooding. So he needed to carry a light jacket with him to school, a umbrella in his backpack, and yet the sky would be a deep blue at dawn.

Spring in Cologne: Time to Play Outside

As I said before, one of the nicest things about spring is that we can go outside again. I loved to walk through the park, to look for flowers and to play with my friends in the garden. In spring we sometimes only had a short rain shower in the afternoon, but there were also days with non-stop rain for hours. Sometimes the ground would dry out again and we could play outside. Other times the sidewalks and streets would be flooded with puddles of water. Because of sudden wind and more intense rainfall, we sometimes had to change our plans. Spring feels like fresh air, muddy shoes and longer days with more time to play outside.

02

Summer

Long evenings by the Rhine

Summer in Cologne: Warm Days and Sudden Storms

Summer is my favorite season and for me it is also the season when I was in elementary school. As I mentioned before the temperatures during spring were around 5°C in the morning and 15-20°C in the afternoon. During summer the temperatures were more or less the same during the whole day. In summer it can get very sticky and even hot but it can also rain a lot. The rain is mostly showers or even thunderstorms that can appear very quickly. They can bring a lot of wind and even loud thunder. I remember very well the thunderstorms during summer because they were always a big change to the warm weather before.

Summer in Cologne: Holidays and Outdoor Memories

Also summer was mostly very wonderful for me. Since I spent most of my time with family and friends, we were always „out and about” during our summer holidays. We went for walks along the Rhine, had bike rides and even went to the zoo once. We stopped off at ice cream shops to buy our favorite flavors and since the weather was mostly very dry and warm, we were able to spend lots of time outside. I remember being satisfied with the days that had dry and warm weather. But when thunderstorms came, we rushed back inside the house in no time at all. Shortly after the rain had stopped, there were big puddles on the ground. So even on the sunniest of days, it could rain suddenly again.

03

Autumn

Golden leaves & rainy walks

Autumn in Cologne: Cooler Air and Falling Leaves

As for Autumn, it was a great season to spend time outdoors with family and friends but as I got older, it wasn’t as fun because school started again. The temperatures would be quite mild in September and drop gradually to around 15-20°C during the days and 5-10°C at night time by late Autumn. It was very drizzly and normally quite wet with slippery leaves on the ground. On some mornings there would be a lot of mist and a gray sky. The season felt quite quiet and I would always look forward to Summer again.

Autumn in Cologne: Windy Days and Cozy Afternoons

One of my favorite activities was collecting leaves and sometimes even collecting chestnuts during our walks. If the wind was blowing strong, then the leaves would swirl around in the streets and in the parks and it felt so cool. Most of the time though the days would be gray and rainy. The wind would be blowing strong and it would be hard to open the umbrella. I remember many days where I had to change my plans because of the rain. After our long walks I loved going back to my cozy home and sit by the fire place.

04

Winter

Cold mornings & rare snowfall

Winter in Cologne: More Rain Than Snow

Also in the winter season Cologne offered a host of possibilities to the child in the picture. But it was a cold, damp, and in many ways grey winter. The temperatures mostly were between 0°C and 7°C, but fell on many nights below freezing point. The ground then was covered with a layer of frost and the car of the family as well as front and back gardens were stiff and crunchy with it. I best remember rain in the winter months, rather than snow, which only occasionally fell in Cologne in the 1960s. It did so lightly and for only a short while, and then would melt away quickly. As a result the occasional frosty morning was often sufficient to make the paths slippery and for us children to have to walk carefully to school.

Winter in Cologne: Small Moments of Excitement

It didn’t have to snow a lot to create a lot of joy. I enjoyed just walking on it, in my tracks, and making a few snowballs. After a while they’d melt but that didn’t matter. The coldest spells were the most noticable. When it had frozen the night before, the ground would still be slippery in the morning and it would take a while to thaw. All in all, a quite varied and colorful season – enhanced by all the lights, and by all the wonderful Christmas markets, where you could find all sorts of warm and tasty drinks. My winter memories are of cold hands and feet, of soaked boots, and of hope for more snow the next morning.

One childhood, four different rhythms.
